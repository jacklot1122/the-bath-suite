Send enquiries to our own thank-you page and fire the Ads conversion there

The forms posted to FormSubmit with no _next, so after a send people landed on
FormSubmit's page, off the domain. There was no page on thebathsuite.com where
a conversion could ever fire, which is why Google Ads kept reporting
"Conversion tracking setup is incomplete" no matter what was configured in the
account.

Adds thanks.html and points every form at it via _next. The Enquiry Form
Submission event snippet (AW-18442373819/xZbZCPKOz_ccELuVgdpE) sits on that
page, so a view means an enquiry genuinely went through. It is noindex, since
indexing it would let people reach it without submitting and inflate the count.

The base Google tag was only on the home and landing pages. It is now on
about, contact, gallery and services too - contact being the one that actually
takes enquiries.

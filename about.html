About the studio

A small studio, deliberately.

Design and construction under one roof, working on one kind of room — and licensed to build it.

Our story

We removed the gap in the middle.

The Bath Suite exists because of a gap most people meet halfway through a renovation. The designer has finished and left. The builder is interpreting a drawing they did not draw. And the client is standing between them, translating — usually on a Tuesday, usually about something that has already been tiled.

We removed the gap by doing both. The person who draws the arched niche is the person accountable for setting it out, so there is no handover to fall through and nobody to point at.

The work is bathrooms, and very nearly only bathrooms. It is the smallest, wettest, most heavily serviced room in a house — six trades have to agree inside a few square metres, and every millimetre is visible when it is finished. Specialising means we have made these decisions before: where the waste can move to, whether the ceiling will take a recessed shelf, what a marble mitre will do at an arch. We make them faster, and with fewer surprises for you.

How we work

Decisions first. Demolition second.

Resolve it on screen

Layout, finishes, fixtures and lighting are settled and rendered in 3D before anything is ordered — the cheapest place to change your mind.

Build it in sequence

Strip-out, structural work, rough-in, waterproofing, screed, tile, joinery, glass, fit-off. In that order, by one team, with no waiting on a subcontractor to call back.

Finish it properly

Silicone lines, grout colour, cabinet alignment, the last brass fitting. The final five per cent is what makes a bathroom look expensive.

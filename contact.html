Before you enquire

A few things worth knowing.

How long does a bathroom take?

A full renovation is usually a matter of weeks rather than days, and the honest answer depends on the room, the structural work and how long your chosen tile and tapware take to arrive. We give you a program with the quote, and we tell you early if a selection has a long lead time.

Do you handle design as well as building?

Yes — that is the point of the studio. Layout, finishes, fixtures and 3D renders are done in house, then we build what we drew. You can also come to us with a designer's plans and we will build to them.

Do I need to be out of the house?

Usually not. If the bathroom is your only one, we will talk through the sequence and the days without water before you commit, so you can plan around it.

Are you licensed and can I check?

Yes, and please do. Work is carried out under NSW Contractor Licence 697736, held by Winspear Developments Pty Ltd, covering Carpenter and Kitchen, Bathroom and Laundry Renovator. The full details are on our About page.
